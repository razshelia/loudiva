LISA

LISA, renowned as a member of BLACKPINK, reaches far beyond the group with her global impact in music and fashion.

She holds multiple Guinness World Records and has won Best K-pop at both the MTV Video Music Awards and the MTV Europe Music Awards.

Each new project pushes further, and her journey keeps setting the pace for global entertainment.

IVE

IVE is a six-member group, there are Yujin, Gaeul, Rei, Wonyoung, Liz, and Leeseo.

They debuted in December 2021 with Eleven and followed with hits like Love Dive, After Like, I Am, and Baddie.

In February 2026 they returned with their second studio album, REVIVE+, the title tracks are Blackhole and Bang Bang.
